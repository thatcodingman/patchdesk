What changed

▲ Added / changed

  • The Illuminate faction, with two new enemy types: the Wretch and the Crusher
  • The Void — a new environment on planets pulled out of normal space, no stars, mutated flora
  • The Gateway — a wormhole opened on Rirga Bay giving access to the Void
  • Randomized primary objectives every operation, replacing the previous more linear mission structure
  • SEAF support squads, secondary extraction zones, and a new tactical video camera tool
  • New mission types: reconnaissance and Illuminate structure destruction

▼ Changed / replaced

  • The old linear, fixed-objective mission structure on Void operations specifically

Was this actually asked for?

Split answer. Several respondents specifically told us the community had been waiting close to six months to actually enter the Void once it first appeared — that part was genuinely anticipated, not a surprise. Where it splits is the enemies themselves: several players expected "actual squids" — proper Illuminate psionic/tech units — and instead got enemies built around the existing Voteless (zombie-style) archetype, which reads to them as reused material dressed up as new content. Others pushed back on that framing entirely, pointing out that new-faction content is rarely something the community explicitly requests in advance, and reported a generally positive, low-complaint reception regardless.

"
It's like if we waited 6 months for a new bot major update only for the new bot enemies to be 2 red terminids.
Helldivers 2 community member — Discord, shared anonymously

Another Discord community member put the wait more bluntly (shared anonymously): "we've been waiting almost 6 months to go in the void and see what's inside, and all we got was a faster voteless and a giant regenerating bullet sponge." A separate community member, also anonymized, framed the letdown differently — not the quality of what shipped, but the quantity: this is, by their account, the fewest new units any major faction update has added yet, for the faction they felt needed reinforcement the most.

File size & rollout

The Devoid of Liberty patch itself was a 5.46 GB download on PS5, going live at 2:01 a.m. PT on Aug 12. Two real rollout issues showed up on launch morning: server strain from launch-day traffic (expected for a major faction drop), and a specific PS5 bug where relaunching the game after updating threw an "Access token is expired" error (JSON error code 2097666) — the fix that worked for affected players was a full PS5 restart and re-authenticating via the QR code prompt. For broader context, total install size currently sits around 35–40 GB on PS5/Xbox; PC was historically far larger (peaked near 154 GB) before a late-2025 optimization pass brought it down to roughly 23 GB.

The long view

Step back far enough and this reads like a good move in the right direction — just not the home run some players were hoping for. Almost all of the drag here comes from one place: the Illuminate side of the content. Two new enemy types is the thinnest roster any major faction update has shipped, and "more voteless, not actual squids" isn't just a cute complaint — it's the actual gap between what got promised and what got delivered. The campaign wrapping in about a week and a build-up that felt lighter than past arcs both point the same direction: the faction players had been waiting on longest got the least room to become something new.

None of that erases what shipped well, though. The systems side — randomized objectives, SEAF squads, the new biome, the presentation — moved the game forward in ways that matter beyond just this one faction. That's the actual long view: this is an incremental improvement, not a transformative one, and the disappointment is real but narrow. It's concentrated almost entirely on what the Illuminate specifically got, not on the update as a whole.

The verdict

Solid step forward for Helldivers 2 overall — better systems, better presentation, real new content on the side. But the Illuminate, the faction that's been waiting the longest for real attention, walked away with the thinnest slice of it. Good update. Not the one the squids actually deserved yet.
