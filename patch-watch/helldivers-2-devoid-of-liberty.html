What changed

▲ Added / changed

  • The Illuminate faction, with two new enemy types: the Wretch and the Crusher
  • The Void — a new environment on planets pulled out of normal space, no stars, mutated flora
  • The Gateway — a wormhole opened on Rirga Bay giving access to the Void
  • Randomized primary objectives every operation, replacing the previous more linear mission structure
  • SEAF support squads, secondary extraction zones, and a new tactical video camera tool
  • New mission types: reconnaissance and Illuminate structure destruction

▼ Removed / nerfed

  • The old linear, fixed-objective mission structure on Void operations specifically

Was this actually asked for?

Split answer. Nearly every respondent independently confirmed the community had been waiting close to six months to actually enter the Void once it first appeared — that part was genuinely anticipated, not a surprise. Where it splits is the enemies themselves: several players expected "actual squids" — proper Illuminate psionic/tech units — and instead got enemies built around the existing Voteless (zombie-style) archetype, which reads to them as reused material dressed up as new content. Others pushed back on that framing entirely, pointing out that new-faction content is rarely something the community explicitly requests in advance, and reported a generally positive, low-complaint reception regardless.

"
It's like if we waited 6 months for a new bot major update only for the new bot enemies to be 2 red terminids.
A player in the community — shared anonymously

Another player put the wait more bluntly: "we've been waiting almost 6 months to go in the void and see what's inside, and all we got was a faster voteless and a giant regenerating bullet sponge." A separate respondent framed the letdown differently — not the quality of what shipped, but the quantity: this is, by their account, the fewest new units any major faction update has added yet, for the faction they felt needed reinforcement the most.
