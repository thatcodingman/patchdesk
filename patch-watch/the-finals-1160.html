What changed

▲ Added / changed

  • AKM damage increased 20 → 21
  • Dagger, Riot Shield, and Spear all got lunge distance/duration and damage buffs
  • Orbital Hitters LTM returns (Sledgehammer-focused, low-gravity combat)
  • Deep Signal event live with a 20+ item reward wheel
  • Engine upgraded from Unreal Engine 5.3 to 5.7
  • Anti-cheat rule fixed — false bans for gyro-controller users being reversed automatically

▼ Removed / nerfed

  • M11: +75% hip-fire recoil, +25% ADS recoil
  • M11: +25% ADS dispersion grounded, +120% airborne
  • KS-23 damage falloff and effective range reduced

What did the community feedback we found say?

In the pre-patch Reddit discussions we found, players had been asking for M11 accuracy/bloom restrictions for a while, and the post-patch reaction leaned toward "finally." AKM buffs were also requested, although there's no strong evidence players specifically wanted +1 damage — some are now questioning whether that's enough.

The melee changes track with community complaints as well. Pre-patch Spear discussions we found had already flagged weak lunge distance and frustration with the stamina-gated Spin Attack — this patch addresses the first problem more than the second.

"
Before rework stamina was not a thing, so you could spin however you like. Now you only get one spin. Before you had to emote-cancel as tech, which stopped your animation, but now you have to land each part of your spin to continue to the next one, which is a 3-part animation, and yeah it cost a lot of fights. Sliding into lunge feels amazing though, and you can chase and set up ambushes a lot more now.
@moozzarela — Spear main, community feedback, Discord twitch.tv/moozzarela

That same player identified Hammer as a particularly bad matchup for Spear — "fighting Hammer is get out or slide like dodge rolling a dark souls boss." Our broader research found similar concerns in Spear-focused discussion, though there isn't enough evidence to call Hammer a universal counter to all melee weapons, just a bad matchup for Spear specifically.

A high-level read

Separate from the Spear-focused feedback above, we also heard from a player with a more competitive/high-level view of the meta, and their read complicates the "Embark is just working through a list" framing.

"
M11 is dead at high level play, it didn't fit well in invis meta and not having 3 dashes already hurt it, making it bloom in air just pushed it to the edge where we probably won't see M11 used in a future dash meta. KS nerf is good but definitely feels they went in a wrong direction for it — main issue was the RPM, for the gun high dmg with good RPM is a recipe for disaster. Demat changes are fantastic but unfortunately Medium still doesn't have good enough util or weapons to capitalize off of it, AKM buff helped but as long as FCAR and FAMAS exist AKM just exists to fill in a weapon slot.
Anonymous — high-level/competitive player, community feedback

This is a meaningfully different read from our Spear main's ground-level experience — where M11 usage was simply "seeing less," one high-level respondent considers M11 effectively nonviable at the highest level, tied to how it interacts with the invisibility meta and dash availability rather than the raw numbers alone. On the KS-23, this respondent argues Embark nerfed the wrong stat — the gun's real problem was its RPM-to-damage ratio, not falloff/range. And on AKM, their view is blunter than the general "positive but modest" community read: as long as FCAR and FAMAS remain dominant, AKM's buff doesn't change its role as a filler pick.

"
Melee changes are good, hopefully there more consistent, and the bow buff is a ticking time bomb for when dash returns. As for the feeling Embark is going down a list, I wouldn't say it necessarily is — it's more trying to get Medium back in comp meta, but it isn't working.
Anonymous — high-level/competitive player, community feedback

Note: we could not verify a bow change in the official 11.6.0 patch notes — the "bow buff" reference above is the respondent's own, included as part of their direct quote rather than a confirmed change we're independently reporting.

That last point is worth sitting with. It's a direct challenge to the "correction patch" framing this piece leans on — one high-level respondent interpreted the patch as an attempt to make Medium viable in competitive play again, and argued that it has not worked. Both readings can be true at once: the patch clearly responds to specific community-named problems (which is what our broader research found), while also, per this respondent's read, not actually solving the deeper class-balance problem it may have been aimed at.

File size & rollout

Xbox saw a delayed rollout on launch day while other platforms already had the update live — crossplay was reportedly still functional in the meantime, per player reports. No strong evidence of a major sustained outage turned up in our research; the official notes mention a fix for a rare server crash tied to network packet acknowledgement, not a rollout-wide issue. Download size wasn't independently verified per platform, so we're not publishing a number rather than guess at one.

The long view

Our research pointed toward 11.6.0 reading like Embark working through a list of problems the community had already been vocal about — M11 was crowding out other SMGs, AKM was underperforming, and melee couldn't reliably close distance. But one high-level respondent interpreted the patch as an attempt to make Medium viable in competitive play again — and argued that it has not worked. Both things can be true — the patch responds to community-named problems while still missing the deeper class-balance goal it may have been built around, per that respondent's read.

What it doesn't do is settle anything. Early reports and the high-level feedback both suggest one high-level respondent considers M11 effectively nonviable at the highest level rather than landing in the middle — worse for competitive relevance than for the average lobby. Melee's core complaint — the stamina/animation system itself, not just target access — is still unresolved. The engine upgrade to UE5.7 is also too fresh to judge confidently; the first-day performance reports in our research were themselves mixed. This is a patch that fixes symptoms the community named specifically, which is worth crediting, but it leaves the harder structural questions — Medium's viability chief among them — on the table for next time.

The verdict

A correction patch that addresses several real community-flagged problems but leaves deeper balance questions open. Embark addressed real, community-flagged problems with M11, AKM, and melee target access — that's a genuine step forward. But one high-level respondent considers M11 effectively nonviable at the highest level, and Spear's stamina-gated Spin Attack remains a deeper complaint that this patch does not address. Whether 11.6.0 actually improves Medium's competitive viability — which one high-level respondent interpreted as a major goal of the patch — remains an open question.
