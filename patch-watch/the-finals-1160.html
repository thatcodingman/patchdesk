What changed

▲ Added / changed

  • AKM damage increased 20 → 21
  • Dagger, Riot Shield, and Spear all got lunge distance/duration and damage buffs
  • Orbital Hitters LTM returns (Sledgehammer-focused, low-gravity combat)
  • Deep Signal event live with a 20+ item reward wheel
  • Engine upgraded from Unreal Engine 5.3 to 5.7
  • Anti-cheat rule fixed — false bans for gyro-controller users being reversed automatically

▼ Removed / nerfed

  • M11: +75% hip-fire recoil, +25% ADS recoil
  • M11: +25% ADS dispersion grounded, +120% airborne
  • KS-23 damage falloff and effective range reduced

Was this actually asked for?

Mostly, yes — on direction, if not on every exact number. Pre-patch Reddit threads had been asking for M11 accuracy/bloom restrictions for a while, and the post-patch reaction leaned strongly toward "finally." AKM buffs were requested too, though there's no strong evidence anyone was specifically asking for +1 damage — some players are now questioning whether that's enough.

The melee changes track with community complaints as well. Multiple pre-patch Spear discussions had already flagged weak lunge distance and frustration with the stamina-gated Spin Attack — this patch addresses the first problem more than the second.

"
Before rework stamina was not a thing, so you could spin however you like. Now you only get one spin. Before you had to emote-cancel as tech, which stopped your animation, but now you have to land each part of your spin to continue to the next one, which is a 3-part animation, and yeah it cost a lot of fights. Sliding into lunge feels amazing though, and you can chase and set up ambushes a lot more now.
Spear main — community feedback, Discord

That same player identified Hammer as a particularly bad matchup for Spear — "fighting Hammer is get out or slide like dodge rolling a dark souls boss" — and that's not an isolated take. Multiple separate Spear-focused threads point to Sledge/Hammer as a real problem matchup, though there isn't enough evidence to call Hammer a universal counter to all melee weapons, just a bad one for Spear specifically.

File size & rollout

Xbox saw a delayed rollout on launch day while other platforms already had the update live — crossplay reportedly stayed functional in the meantime. There's no strong evidence of a major sustained outage; the official notes mention a fix for a rare server crash tied to network packet acknowledgement, not a rollout-wide issue. Download size wasn't independently verified per platform, so we're not publishing a number rather than guess at one.

The long view

11.6.0 reads less like a meta reset and more like Embark working through a list of problems the community had already been vocal about — M11 was crowding out other SMGs, AKM was underperforming, and melee couldn't reliably close distance. That's a good sign for where the game is headed: it suggests the balance team is actually listening to matchup and weapon-viability complaints rather than chasing hype changes.

What it doesn't do is settle anything. Early reports suggest M11 may have swung from overtuned to inconsistent rather than landing in the middle, and melee's core complaint — the stamina/animation system itself, not just target access — is still unresolved. The engine upgrade to UE5.7 is also too fresh to judge; performance reports are genuinely mixed right now. This is a patch that fixes symptoms the community named specifically, which is worth crediting, but it leaves the harder structural questions on the table for next time.

The verdict

A correction patch, not a full fix. Embark addressed real, community-flagged problems with M11, AKM, and melee target access — that's a genuine step forward. But the M11 nerf may have overcorrected, and Spear's stamina-gated Spin Attack remains the deeper issue nobody's touched yet.
