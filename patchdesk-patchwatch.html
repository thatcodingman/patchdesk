How we grade Patch Watch

Different game, different rubric

A launch-day Review asks "is it worth the price." Patch Watch asks something different — did this actually get better, and does that match what players think? Same five checks, every time.

01

What changed

The real delta — what got added, changed, or quietly removed, laid out plainly instead of buried in patch notes.

02

Was it requested?

Was this something players were actually asking for — on Reddit, Discord, forums — or a surprise nobody called for?

03

Community sentiment

How the community actually reacted, scored separately from our own verdict — the two aren't always the same.

04

File size & rollout

Download size per platform, and any install or server issues that came with it.

05

The long view

Our honest take: was this actually good for where the game is headed, and what might it push the developers into down the line?

The Patch Desk publishes independent game reviews. No score is bought or sponsored — if that ever changes for a specific piece of coverage, it'll be disclosed on the article itself.
