Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: AUG 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what information The Patch Desk collects when you visit the site, comment on a review, or use the contact form.

2. Contact form data

If you use the contact form, the name, email, and message you submit are sent to us via Netlify Forms so we can respond. This data isn't sold or shared with third parties.

3. Comments

Comments are powered by Cusdis, a third-party comment service. Cusdis does not require an account to comment, and its own privacy practices govern any data submitted through the comment widget. No login or personal account is required to comment on this site.

4. Analytics

The Patch Desk may use an analytics service to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which reviews are read most. This data is aggregated where possible and isn't used to identify individual visitors.

5. Advertising cookies

If the site displays third-party advertising in the future, the ad provider may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or your ad provider's opt-out tools.

6. Embedded video

Reviews may eventually embed YouTube videos. Embedded videos can set their own cookies per YouTube's privacy policy once that content is live.

7. Children's privacy

The Patch Desk isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@thepatchdesk.com or through the contact page.

The Patch Desk publishes independent game reviews. No score is bought or sponsored — if that ever changes for a specific piece of coverage, it'll be disclosed on the article itself.
